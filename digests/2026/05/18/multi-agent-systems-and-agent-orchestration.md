## Analysis

The multi-agent orchestration landscape is experiencing a significant platform war as major cloud providers compete for enterprise control plane dominance. This represents a strategic shift from model competition to infrastructure control, with enterprises now choosing between Anthropic's unified platform versus best-of-breed orchestration frameworks.

This addresses enterprise concerns about predictability and governance in production environments. The simultaneous emergence of autonomous payment capabilities in AWS AgentCore suggests the industry is moving beyond simple task orchestration toward commercial agent ecosystems where AI agents can independently engage in economic transactions.
