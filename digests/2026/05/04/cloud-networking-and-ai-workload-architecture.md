## Trend Reflection

**Summary:** Infrastructure capacity scaling has accelerated dramatically with OpenAI achieving 10GW capacity (50% of 2029 target) three years early, while post-quantum security requirements for AI systems have emerged as an immediate architectural imperative. Traditional enterprise networks are officially failing to support AI workloads, marking a definitive shift from incremental optimization to fundamental architectural transformation.

**Key Deltas:** 
- **Hyperscale Infrastructure Achievement**: OpenAI's 3GW capacity addition in 90 days represents unprecedented scaling velocity, validating purpose-built AI infrastructure over adapted cloud architectures
- **Security Architecture Evolution**: Post-quantum cryptographic agility has shifted from future consideration to present-day requirement for AI infrastructure, with MCP deployments requiring immediate PQC-ready transport layers
- **Enterprise Network Crisis Confirmation**: IDC's 2026 report formally identifies traditional network architectures as inadequate for AI workloads, escalating from April's "infrastructure pressure" to May's "complexity crisis"
- **AI-Native Infrastructure Emergence**: The Stargate facility model establishes new paradigm combining Oracle Cloud Infrastructure with NVIDIA GB200 systems, moving beyond traditional cloud provider boundaries

**Velocity:** High interest shift - The industry has progressed from identifying AI networking bottlenecks (April 2026) to delivering concrete hyperscale solutions and acknowledging fundamental architectural inadequacy of legacy systems within the 48-hour window.
