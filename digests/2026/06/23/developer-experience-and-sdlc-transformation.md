## Trend Reflection

**Summary:** Platform Engineering 2.0 has formally emerged as the architectural response to AI-native development, transforming IDPs into Agentic Development Platforms with GPU orchestration and agent lifecycle management. Quality assurance challenges have escalated from theoretical concerns to production crises, with financial institutions facing the "looks right" problem where AI-generated code bypasses traditional validation frameworks.

**Key Deltas:**
- VMware and PlatformEngineering.org officially launched Platform Engineering 2.0 framework (June 16), formalizing the transition from IDPs to ADPs with multi-persona support beyond developers
- GitHub Copilot desktop app reached general availability (June 17) with agent sandboxes, while platform processes 14 billion commits annually vs. 1 billion in 2025
- Quality assurance "looks right" problem crystallized as systemic risk in financial services, with QA teams unable to validate AI-generated code at scale
- Platform-as-Product methodology gained renewed emphasis as most IDPs fail due to infrastructure-first rather than product-first approaches
- AI coding tools landscape consolidated around Cursor, Claude Code, and Lovable, with parallel agent capabilities emerging in newer tools like Backgrind

**Velocity:** High interest shift
