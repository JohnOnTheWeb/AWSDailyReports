## Trend Reflection

**Summary:** Platform engineering has transitioned from emerging practice to mainstream enterprise adoption with formal product management approaches, while AI coding measurement challenges have crystallized into industry-wide ROI visibility crisis. The convergence represents maturation of both platform discipline and recognition that AI productivity gains require entirely new measurement frameworks.

**Key Deltas:** Amazon Q Developer GitLab Ultimate integration extends agentic capabilities beyond IDE to full SDLC orchestration; platform engineering adoption shifted from tactical DevOps renaming to strategic product management with lifecycle ownership; AI coding ROI measurement crisis formalized through industry reports warning of misleading dashboards despite high developer satisfaction; DORA metrics evolved specifically for AI-assisted development with reliability patterns identified; Datadog launched AI Impact platform connecting telemetry to delivery outcomes, representing first major vendor response to measurement gap.

**Velocity:** Medium interest shift
