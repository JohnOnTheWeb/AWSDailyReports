## Trend Reflection

**Summary:** Enterprise agentic AI platforms have reached full integration within business application stacks, exemplified by Oracle's AI-native builder for Fusion Applications enabling both business users and developers to create production AI agents. The measurement framework crisis continues deepening as traditional DORA metrics prove increasingly inadequate for AI-augmented development workflows.

**Key Deltas:** 
- Oracle's July 14 announcement represents the first major ERP-native agentic development platform, moving beyond standalone tools to embedded enterprise infrastructure
- Claude Code achieved 71% market share among regular AI agent users with 80.8% SWE-bench performance, establishing clear market leadership
- AWS expanded Amazon Q Developer with multi-agent capabilities (/doc, /test, /review agents) beyond the original /dev agent
- Industry recognition that DORA metrics capture less than half of developer activities in AI-assisted workflows, driving demand for comprehensive engineering intelligence platforms
- GitHub Copilot productivity claims validated at 75% higher job satisfaction and 55% code productivity improvements without quality degradation

**Velocity:** Medium interest shift
