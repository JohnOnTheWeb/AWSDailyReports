The completion of AI agent sandbox rollouts across all major cloud providers establishes enterprise-grade agentic infrastructure as a reality rather than roadmap item.

**Key Deltas:** 
- **AMD Helios Production Reality**: Transitioned from April-June 2026 roadmap discussions to confirmed gigawatt-scale production deployments with OpenAI, Meta, and Anthropic partnerships
