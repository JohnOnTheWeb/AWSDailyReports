## Analysis

The September 13-14, 2026 window reveals accelerating enterprise adoption of multi-agent orchestration platforms, with market projections suggesting massive growth potential driven by subscription-based deployment models. The simultaneous rise of open-source alternatives like CorvinOS suggests a bifurcated market between enterprise managed services and developer-controlled implementations.
